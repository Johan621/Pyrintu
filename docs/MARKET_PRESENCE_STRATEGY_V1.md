# Pyrintu — Market Presence & Go-to-Market Strategy v1.0

## Status
Drafted as the first market-presence operating specification. This document guides brand presence, discovery, SEO, social media, community building, launch, measurement, and India-wide expansion. It does not authorize paid acquisition or public launch until the product and validation gates are passed.

## 1. Objective
Build a trusted, recognizable Indian consumer brand around Pyrintu's core promise:

> Help compatible people become mutually invested real-world relationships.

The market system must create four outcomes:
1. Discovery — people find Pyrintu.
2. Trust — people understand what Pyrintu is and why it is safe.
3. Activation — people complete the first meaningful connection flow.
4. Retention/referral — successful relationships create repeat use and organic growth.

## 2. Market Position
Primary positioning:

> Pyrintu helps people build meaningful real-world relationships through compatible people, small groups, shared experiences, and mutual follow-through.

Core differentiator:
- Mutuality Engine: optimize for reciprocal willingness, not superficial match volume.
- Relationship Momentum: help promising connections continue after the first interaction.

Do not position Pyrintu as:
- a dating app;
- a generic events marketplace;
- a swipe/feed social network;
- an AI chatbot;
- an "AI friend" replacement for humans.

## 3. India Launch Strategy
Launch city: Hyderabad.

Expansion sequence should be evidence-driven rather than calendar-driven. A city is eligible for expansion only when the previous city demonstrates healthy supply/demand density, successful first meetings, recurring interactions, safety performance, and sustainable acquisition.

Suggested sequence to evaluate after Hyderabad:
1. Bengaluru
2. Pune
3. Chennai
4. Mumbai
5. Delhi NCR
6. Other high-density student/young-professional cities

These are candidate expansion markets, not commitments.

## 4. Brand System
Brand principles:
- Human, not robotic.
- Warm, not childish.
- Intelligent, not technical for its own sake.
- Premium, not luxury-for-show.
- Safe, not fearful.
- India-native, not stereotype-heavy.

Core message hierarchy:
1. Real relationships.
2. Mutual effort.
3. Small groups and shared experiences.
4. AI that helps, never replaces human choice.
5. Safety and trust.

## 6. SEO Strategy
SEO will follow Google's people-first guidance rather than large-scale search-engine-first content generation. Google states that the same foundational SEO practices apply to AI Overviews and AI Mode; there are no special AI-search markup requirements. Pages must be crawlable/indexable, internally linked, useful, textual where appropriate, and supported by accurate structured data. [Source: Google Search Central]

Priority SEO assets:
- Hyderabad-specific landing page.
- High-intent pages about meeting people in Hyderabad through activities.
- Genuine guides based on Pyrintu's observed product/community experience.
- Activity pages only when they represent real Pyrintu-supported experiences.
- City expansion pages only after launch readiness.
- Founder/company credibility pages.

Avoid:
- 100s of AI-generated location pages.
- Keyword stuffing.
- Thin "best X in Y" pages without original value.
- Publishing articles only because a keyword has search volume.

Every SEO page should have a primary user purpose and a reason to exist beyond ranking.

Technical SEO baseline:
- HTTPS
- canonical URLs
- robots.txt
- XML sitemap
- clean information architecture
- descriptive titles/meta descriptions
- Open Graph/Twitter cards
- structured data where appropriate and supported
- image alt text
- fast, mobile-first page experience
- Search Console
- Analytics
- event/conversion tracking

## 7. AI Search / Generative Search Presence
Google's current guidance says traditional SEO fundamentals remain the basis for eligibility in AI Overviews and AI Mode. Pyrintu should therefore publish authoritative, original, first-party material rather than trying to optimize for an artificial "GEO" checklist.

The target is to become a source that answers questions such as:
- How can I make friends after moving to Hyderabad?
- How do adults make genuine friends in a new city?
- How do activity-based friendship groups work?
- How can I meet people without dating apps?
- How do you know whether a new friendship is mutual?

Pyrintu should answer these with original research, product learnings, safety guidance, and anonymized aggregate insights where appropriate.

## 8. Social Media Portfolio
Primary channels for consumer growth:
- Instagram — primary discovery and brand/emotion channel.
- YouTube — deeper storytelling, explainers, founder content, community stories.
- WhatsApp — conversion/community operations, not a public content channel only.
- LinkedIn — founder/company credibility, partnerships, hiring, ecosystem presence.

Secondary/experimental:
- X
- Reddit/community participation where appropriate and non-spammy.

Do not attempt to post everywhere from day one. Content quality and consistency matter more than channel count.

## 9. Content Pillars
Pyrintu's content should reinforce the product thesis.

Pillar A — The Social Problem
Examples:
- Why making friends gets harder after college.
- Why having many contacts can still feel lonely.
- Why matches die after the first conversation.

Pillar B — Real-world connection
- Activity ideas.
- Small-group formats.
- How to start conversations.
- How to turn acquaintance into friendship.

Pillar C — Build in Public
- Building Pyrintu in India.
- Product decisions.
- Experiments and lessons.
- Founder/engineering journey.

Pillar D — Trust & Safety
- How Pyrintu protects users.
- Why we avoid engagement traps.
- Privacy principles.
- Healthy community behavior.

Pillar E — Community stories
Only publish with informed consent and appropriate anonymization.

## 10. Content Cadence — Initial Target
Before product-market validation:
- Instagram: 3–5 high-quality posts/reels per week.
- YouTube: 1 substantive video every 1–2 weeks.
- LinkedIn: 2–3 high-quality company/founder posts per week.
- Website: 1 genuinely useful article/resource per week maximum; quality over volume.

Cadence can change from analytics. It is not a KPI by itself.

## 11. Founder Brand
The founder should be visible, but Pyrintu must not become dependent on one personality.

Founder content themes:
- Why Pyrintu exists.
- Building an Indian consumer technology company.
- Product/engineering thinking.
- Lessons from experiments.
- Community observations.

Company accounts remain the canonical brand voice.

Candidate acquisition communities:

- shared activity invitation;

Measure each transition.

Pyrintu LinkedIn goals:

- hiring;
- partnerships;
- founder story;
- India startup ecosystem visibility.

## 16. Instagram / Short-form Video
Meta reported in 2026 that short-form video was a dominant format in India, with 97% of surveyed consumers watching short-form video daily and 60% of time on Facebook and Instagram spent on video. This supports Reels as a primary discovery channel for Pyrintu, but not as a substitute for product quality. [Source: Meta/Retailers Association of India report]

Content style:
- short, emotionally recognizable social situations;
- "you just moved to a new city" moments;
- friendship psychology;
- authentic product-building footage;
- community stories;
- Hyderabad-local content.

Avoid:
- generic motivational content;
- AI-generated fake people/testimonials;
- engagement bait;
- pretending to have a community that does not yet exist.

## 17. Press & PR
Initial PR should focus on a credible company narrative:

> An Indian startup building a trust-centered AI product for real-world relationships.

Potential story angles after real evidence exists:
- Hyderabad launch.
- First community outcomes.
- Original relationship research.
- Safety innovations.
- City expansion.

Do not pursue vanity press before product evidence exists.

## 18. Influencer / Creator Strategy
Prefer micro- and nano-creators with strong local trust over celebrity reach in the beginning.

Candidate profiles:
- Hyderabad student creators;
- city-life creators;
- college creators;
- career/young-professional creators;
- hobby/community creators.

Partnership model:
- creator-specific landing links;
- tracked invite codes;
- authentic first-person experience;
- no scripted claims about product outcomes.

## 19. Paid Acquisition
Paid advertising is a later amplifier, not the initial source of truth.

Before meaningful paid spend, Pyrintu must know:
- activation rate;
- cost to qualified user;
- first-meet conversion;
- second-interaction conversion;
- safety/quality metrics;
- retention.

Otherwise paid traffic can hide product-market problems.

Core acquisition metrics:

- organic conversion rate;
- social reach;
- profile visits;
- waitlist/signup conversion;
- activation;
- referral rate.

Core quality metrics:
- first meetup completion;
- mutuality rate;
- second interaction rate;
- relationship continuity;
- safety incident rate.

## 21. Analytics Stack
Initial owned measurement:
- Google Search Console
- privacy-conscious analytics platform / Google Analytics as appropriate
- product event analytics
- social platform analytics
- UTM conventions
- first-party conversion database

Pyrintu's own database remains the source of truth for product outcomes; platform-reported engagement is treated as acquisition evidence, not user-value truth.

## 22. SEO + Social Operating Rule
No content should be created solely because an AI tool says it will rank.

No social post should be published solely to satisfy a posting quota.

Every public asset must support one of:
- awareness;
- trust;
- education;
- community;
- conversion;
- retention/referral.

## 23. Launch Stages
Stage 0 — Private research
- no public claims beyond product thesis.

Stage 1 — Waitlist
- website + social identity + community interviews.

Stage 2 — Closed Hyderabad beta
- invite-only.

Stage 3 — Evidence-led public launch
- publish first real outcomes.

Stage 4 — City growth
- expand only with repeatable city playbook.

Stage 5 — India expansion
- localized city/community acquisition.

## 24. Website/SEO Technical Acceptance Criteria
- Core pages indexable.
- Search Console verified.
- Sitemap submitted.
- No accidental noindex on public pages.
- Canonicals correct.
- Structured data matches visible page content.
- Mobile performance acceptable.
- Core conversion events instrumented.
- Social preview metadata correct.
- 404 and redirect behavior defined.
- Privacy/legal pages accessible.

## 25. Public Launch Gate
Do not call Pyrintu publicly "India's #1" or claim large community/relationship outcomes without evidence.

Before public nationwide positioning, require:
- stable product;
- documented safety process;
- measurable positive relationship outcomes;
- clear privacy model;
- customer-support workflow;
- incident response process;
- analytics dashboard;
- brand assets;
- SEO baseline;
- social content library;
- city launch playbook.
